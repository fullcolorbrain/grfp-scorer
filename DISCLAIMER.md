## 1. What this tool is

GRFP Scorer is an educational writing aid. It compares a draft of an NSF Graduate
Research Fellowship Program statement against a fixed ten-principle writing rubric and
reports how well the draft matches that rubric. That is all it does.

## 2. What the scores mean — and do not mean

- The scores reflect **only how closely a draft matches the rubric**. They are not
  predictions of, and have no bearing on, decisions made by the National Science
  Foundation or any other funder, which are made independently by review panels using
  their own criteria.
- **A high score does not mean an application will be funded. A low score does not mean
  it will not be.** Fellowship outcomes depend on many factors this tool does not and
  cannot evaluate, including letters of recommendation, transcripts, the research plan,
  the applicant pool, and program priorities in a given year.
- The rubric was calibrated on a limited set of past statements, mostly in the life and
  behavioral sciences. Its relevance to other fields, and to future application cycles,
  is not guaranteed.
- When a language model is used as the reader, scores can vary from run to run and
  between models. The tool is a prompt for revision, not a measurement instrument.

## 4. Third-party services and your own API account

- If you choose the Claude backend, the text of your statement is sent to Anthropic's
  API **under an API key that you supply and pay for**. The author provides no API
  access, receives none of your statement text, and is not responsible for any charges
  to your account. Your use of that service is governed by Anthropic's own terms and
  privacy policy, which you should review.
- If you choose the Ollama or offline backend, your statement text does not leave your
  computer.
- Score only drafts you are entitled to share with a third-party service. Do not submit
  other people's statements without their permission.

## 5. Registration and usage information

On first launch the application asks for your name, email address, and institution,
and sends them — together with your operating system, the app version, and a random
installation identifier — to the author, so that the number of users and institutions
can be counted. If you tick the optional box, the application also sends an anonymous
record (the installation identifier, which type of statement was scored, the scores,
which backend read it, and the word count) after each scoring run. **The text of your statement is never sent to the
author.** See PRIVACY.md for details and for how to have your registration deleted.
